Madrid, Spain, and Sofia, Bulgaria - 16th June 2026 - Xcalibur Smart Mapping and Dronamics announce a partnership to develop an unmanned aerial geophysics solution for natural resource exploration over land and oceans. The resulting drone-enabled solution will integrate Xcalibur’s airborne geophysical acquisition system for magnetometry and gravity with Dronamics’ Black Swan unmanned aircraft platform.
