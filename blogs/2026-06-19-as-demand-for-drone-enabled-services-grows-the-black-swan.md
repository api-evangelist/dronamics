Dronamics, both the company and its technology, is built on first principles. It was founded on a simple premise - unmanned aerial systems can inject a much-needed competitive advantage into the economy by improving cost, time and access to aerial services. Only 1% of goods worldwide travel by air, the fastest and also the most expensive way.
